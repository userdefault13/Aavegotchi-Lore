## 💡 Recommended Starting Concept

**"The Protocol Paladin"**

✅ **Identity:** aAAVE maxi from Grid who earned Captain title through crisis leadership  
✅ **Archetype:** Strategic Commander + Charismatic Maverick blend  
✅ **Powers:** Protocol Synergy + Community Empowerment (team-focused)  
✅ **Visual:** Purple armor-robe hybrid, Spirit Blade, ROFL companion  
✅ **Personality:** Confident but humble, protective, uses humor  
✅ **Role:** Supporting Hero & Quest-Giver in Great Battles arc  

**Why This Works:**
- Embodies AAVE protocol values (community, shared risk/reward)
- Relatable hero who earned position vs. chosen one
- Balances serious leadership with Aavegotchi playfulness
- Flexible for expansion based on community feedback
- Meme-able and marketable
